Palvio Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Palvio (“the app”) is a color toolkit for Android. It is published by Webstify, an individual developer based in Pakistan (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Palvio has no accounts, no sign-in and no ads.
  • Palvio has no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no tracking.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. We run no servers for the app.
  • Your photos and your saved palettes stay on your phone.

What stays on your device

The app saves your palettes (the colors you pick or type, and the palette names you choose) and your settings on your phone. This data is never sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear the app’s data in your phone’s settings.

Photos and camera

When you tap “Choose photo”, the app opens your phone’s photo picker and reads the photo you select. When you tap “Take photo”, the app asks for camera permission and opens your phone’s camera. The app reads the color of the pixels you touch. Photos are processed on your device only. They are not uploaded, shared or kept by us, although your phone may hold a temporary copy in the app’s private storage while the photo is open. You can withdraw the camera permission at any time in your phone’s settings; the other features keep working.

Clipboard

When you tap a copy button (for example, to copy palette code), the app writes that text to your clipboard. The app does not read what is on your clipboard.

Purchases

Palvio is free to use with a limit on saved palettes. An optional one-time purchase called “Pro” unlocks more features. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your card or payment details. Google tells the app whether your Google account has bought Pro, so the purchase can be restored if you reinstall or change phones. Google’s own privacy policy applies to purchases and to Google Play.

Internet access

Palvio is designed to work without an internet connection, and it does not send your data to us or to any advertising or analytics company. Your phone, Android and Google Play may collect their own technical data under their own policies, outside our control.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you tap “Take photo”.
  • Photos: the app can only read the photos you pick in the system photo picker.

The app does not use your location, contacts, microphone or any account on your phone.

Children

Palvio is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no personal data, we do not knowingly collect any from children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question or a request about privacy, contact us at the address below and we will reply.

Open-source software

Palvio is built with open-source software, including Expo, React Native, React Native Skia and Ionicons. Their licenses apply to those components.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the effective date above, and update the app’s store listing where needed.
